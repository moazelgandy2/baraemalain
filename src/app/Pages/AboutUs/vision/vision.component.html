Vision

Prepare knowledgeable, pioneering, contemporary and globally minded generations who are capable to innovate, create and compete.



Mission

Baraem Al Ain Private School, through its qualified educational staff, and in partnership with parents and the local community, seeks to provide effective institutional performance that ensures the quality of learning outcomes according to the highest national and international standards, and qualifies a generation that possesses knowledge, skills and values, belongs to the United Arab Emirates, and assumes their social responsibilities in a developing and changing world.
